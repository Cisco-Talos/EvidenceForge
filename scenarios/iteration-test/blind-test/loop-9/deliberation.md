## Key Agreements

- The supplied evidence is technically credible and useful for investigation. The experts retained positive findings about source-native formats, process identities, SSH/SCP transport-before-authentication relationships, separate proxy legs and macOS execution/audit transitions.
- None established a hard contradiction, impossible lifecycle, generator identity leak or source-native schema defect. Passing bounded consistency checks does not establish production provenance; localized distribution artifacts do not establish fabrication.
- All four independently confirmed the exact native fork-time groups during deliberation and treated them as distribution texture with meaningful but capped weight. All also accepted the narrow console distribution, service-byte envelope concern and curl discrepancy with their stated qualifications.
- Windows console placement and the related four-image process vocabulary overlap. They must not be counted as independent proofs merely because many rows repeat them.
- Missing source families, unmatched pre-window initiators, sanitized domains, complete matching and a reconstructable attack narrative were not grounds for adverse scoring. No expert recommended arbitrary retries, invented failures or additional sources solely for completeness.

## Key Disagreements

The original disagreement was mainly about evidentiary weight, not incompatible observations. The Threat Hunter initially gave feasible operational pivots and the absence of contradictions enough weight for a modest Real lean. The other reviewers considered these compatible with careful synthesis. After independently checking endpoint and network distributions, the Threat Hunter accepted that distinction and moved to Inconclusive; the operational findings themselves were retained.

The final verdict is unanimous, but the residual weighting differs. Revised synthetic-confidence ranges from 46 to 54: the Threat Hunter and Detection Engineer remain on the production-like side of the midpoint, while Network and Host/EDR are slightly above it. There is no final categorical Real or Synthetic vote to infer from those scores.

The repeated document reads remain a qualified specialty concern rather than a shared priority. Host/EDR retained tool/action fit as a secondary issue. Threat Hunter emphasized that raw inspection or deliberate collection may be suspicious activity rather than an authenticity defect; Detection and Network assigned little weight absent a stronger contradiction. The original mail-TXT concern was explicitly reduced by Network, and the other reviewers did not elevate it.

## Most Debated Points — Round 2

- **Scheduling intent versus native observation time.** Integral-second command scheduling can occur in automation. The experts found exact fractional preservation at separate native fork observations, including matching monotonic intervals, less ordinary than integral intent alone. Two hosts strengthen the inference; the limited affected population and possible transformations prevent calling it impossible.
- **Consistency versus provenance.** Valid schemas and correlations demonstrate that the visible operations can happen. They do not cancel aggregate texture findings, nor does aggregate texture invalidate the operational reconstruction. This resolved the initial weighting disagreement without inventing a lifecycle defect.
- **Distribution concern versus collection/workload selection.** Shared Windows management can concentrate process populations; real database/directory workloads can overlap in response size. The experts accepted the measured patterns while limiting conclusions about their origin. No post hoc histogram was assigned a fabricated significance level.
- **Suspicious activity versus authenticity defect.** Cat can legitimately inspect or collect binary files, and concentrated TXT requests can result from diagnostics or suspicious DNS activity. Neither is defective merely because it is unusual. Network withdrew the TXT cluster as a remediation priority rather than demanding extra telemetry to explain it.
- **Policy message versus execution outcome.** Host/EDR explicitly refined its MeetingAssist wording to agree with the other reviewers' more limited interpretation: a policy diagnostic alone proves neither successful execution nor a definite rejection. No new adverse indicator followed from the correction.

## Improvement Recommendations (Consensus)

These are conditional recommendations supported by all or most reviewers, not a finding that the collection is synthetic.

1. **Model separate native fork observations with realistic execution/observation variation.** Preserve fork/exec/exit ordering and consistency between wall and monotonic clocks. Do not independently jitter rendered timestamps in ways that break lifecycle relationships. If exact anchor preservation reflects an actual collection transformation, document that transformation rather than inventing an explanation.
2. **Make Windows console and background process distributions follow caller, session and workload semantics.** Validate ownership, parentage and session placement against the represented Windows builds and collection. Tie server starts to concrete role-specific activity while retaining justified shared management behavior; adding arbitrary process noise would not address the identified concern.
3. **Derive MySQL and LDAP bytes from their own operations and result cardinalities.** Preserve application-specific request/result behavior and existing valid packet accounting instead of relying on similarly broad response envelopes. The goal is workload-grounded size variation, not artificially forcing two service histograms apart.
4. **Keep a named curl installation's default identity coherent.** Reflect an intentional binary/configuration transition, override or proxy transformation where that is actually modeled and captured. Preserve uncertainty when the collection cannot establish which explanation applies; absent package auditing alone does not prove an inconsistency in the executable.
5. **Preserve the demonstrated strengths while addressing texture.** Retain scoped identities, transport/authentication separation, proxy control/tunnel accounting, passive TLS visibility, macOS fork/exec distinctions, impersonation context and policy-message semantics. Do not degrade these relationships or add arbitrary failures/source coverage to make the records appear messier.

Document-application choice remains a secondary Host/EDR recommendation: use a tool appropriate to the intended action and retain cat for genuine raw-byte inspection or collection. It is not a panel requirement that every PDF/Office extension be opened through a graphical application. The late mail-TXT cluster is not a consensus improvement priority after deliberation.
