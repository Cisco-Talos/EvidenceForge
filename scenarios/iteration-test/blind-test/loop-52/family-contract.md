# Public subscriber certificate validity by modeled issuance era

Classification: new_family / canonical certificate policy. Owner: TlsCertificatePlanner plus data-driven issuer trust metadata and validated TLS policy catalog. Certificate identity and all SSL/X509/files/OCSP consumers must share one canonical interval. Never truncate a rendered X509 field independently.

Explicit public-trust membership belongs to issuer configuration, not guessed issuer-name substrings or destination suffixes. Private/self-signed and intermediate/root certificates keep their own lifetime rules. Ordinary public leaf generation must use the CA/B Forum operational-period recommendations for its modeled issuance epoch:397 before2026-03-15,199 until2027-03-15,99 until2029-03-15,46 thereafter (hard maximum398/200/100/47). Date policy is data with schema/overlay validation. A newly issued era certificate may be backdated slightly; the issuance anchor, not merely the notBefore or observation date, selects the policy.

Use an order-independent issuance/rotation bucket anchored to each policy's effective date. Preserve historical pre-transition generation, do not mutate an already-resolved certificate when a later handshake is observed, keep the complete event inside its interval and issuer bounds. A policy transition may deliberately rotate certificates. Scoped timing sampling must stay through TimingRuntime. Entry paths: direct public TLS, proxy server leg, baseline/storyline and compatibility planner; sibling private leaf and intermediate generation must remain unchanged. Test each boundary, reverse lookup order, shared identity, historical/private cases and malformed policy catalog through validate-config. No scenario or observation changes.
