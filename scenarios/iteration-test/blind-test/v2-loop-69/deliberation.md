# Deliberation Summary

## Panel Composition

| Expert | Initial Verdict | Initial Verdict Confidence | Initial Synthetic-Confidence | Final Verdict | Final Verdict Confidence | Final Synthetic-Confidence |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|
| Threat Hunter | Inconclusive | 79/100 | 44/100 | Synthetic | 84/100 | 68/100 |
| Detection Engineer | Synthetic | 86/100 | 76/100 | Synthetic | 88/100 | 78/100 |
| Network Forensics | Synthetic | 82/100 | 65/100 | Synthetic | 86/100 | 72/100 |
| Host/EDR Forensics | Synthetic | 84/100 | 72/100 | Synthetic | 87/100 | 77/100 |

The Round 1 presentations explained the initial spread. The Threat Hunter emphasized realistic independent sensor views, disciplined process lifecycles, source-specific collection gaps, and convincing Windows, remote-execution, and proxy pivots, while assigning greatest negative weight to the SQL ordering gap and two remote-service port discontinuities. The Detection Engineer emphasized the foreign `10.10.1.99` address in the local `runas /netonly` Event 4648 chain, the Server 2022-era Event 4698 schema mismatch, the 120-of-120 SMB timestamp lattice, and bounded SSH and Windows-provider delays. The Network Forensics analyst emphasized 29 of 32 proxy-leg pairs ending at the same microsecond and 27 of 59 persistent-HTTP intervals landing within 10 microseconds of 600 ms, while finding packet accounting, DNS, TLS, DHCP, and sensor viewpoints highly credible. The Host/EDR analyst emphasized implausible Postfix worker reuse, repeated application/destination mismatches, fixed daemon quotas, a four-value `SearchFilterHost.exe` argument pool, and new eCAR object IDs for unlocks despite stable logon and session IDs.

In Round 3, the Threat Hunter moved from Inconclusive to Synthetic after the other specialists supplied several independent, population-level fingerprints that were not visible from an attack-lifecycle-centered review. The Threat Hunter reduced the SQL finding from a standalone hard contradiction to strong circumstantial evidence because the FLOW has no process identity, but judged that the verified SMB and HTTP timing lattices plus host identity-state defects outweighed the corpus's substantial realism. The other three experts retained Synthetic verdicts. Detection increased slightly because the network and host findings independently corroborated bounded generation behavior; Network increased after considering the foreign 4648 address and host-state patterns; Host/EDR increased after considering the specialist-confirmed network timing lattices. All retained scores below the confidently-synthetic band because the corpus has unusually strong source-native semantics and internally coherent lifecycles.

## Key Agreements

- The corpus has high source-native fidelity. Windows and Sysmon schemas are mostly credible, Zeek state/history and byte accounting are strong, TLS and X.509 relationships are coherent, Linux services are rendered with substantial detail, and major cross-source pivots are usable.
- Complete correlation is not itself synthetic evidence. The panel treated sensor-local UIDs, differing clock offsets, selective gaps, capture-loss accounting, and non-identical endpoint observations as evidence for realism or as neutral—not as suspicious perfection.
- The strongest synthetic evidence is population-level and contract-specific: exact timing lattices, identity ownership leaks, state-object discontinuity, low-entropy runtime values, and implausible worker reuse. It does not depend on the suspicious activity being compact, linear, clean, or easy to narrate.
- Sparse missing companions are not decisive without a contradictory collection profile. Five missing FUIDs among 1,958 references, one missing TLS companion after STARTTLS, and one SMB read without a visible open can each result from export loss, analyzer behavior, an aborted handshake, or a durable handle.
- The revised panel consensus is Synthetic, with a final mean synthetic-confidence of 73.75/100. This is a likely-synthetic judgment, not a claim that individual records are crude or that a single physical impossibility proves generation.

## Key Disagreements

- The Threat Hunter initially viewed realistic operational coherence as sufficient to remain Inconclusive; the other experts argued that realism at the record and storyline levels can coexist with dataset-wide generator fingerprints. The repeated timing and state distributions carried greater weight because they span many independent records and source families.
- The SQL flow at `13:42:46.992Z` precedes the matching `sqlcmd.exe -S 10.0.2.50` creation at `13:42:59.191Z`, and the exact tuple is independently visible in Zeek at `13:42:47.087020Z`. However, the eCAR FLOW contains no actor or PID, so another process could have generated the failed SYN. The panel treats this as a high-value contract gap requiring ownership repair, not as a conclusively proven physical impossibility.
- The two remote-service Event 4624 source ports do not match the observed SMB transport ports. The Threat Hunter regarded repetition across two tightly timed service-install chains as suspicious; the other experts noted that authentication and service-control traffic can use separate connections and that an unobserved parallel connection remains possible. The finding is retained as medium evidence, not a hard contradiction.
- The Detection Engineer treated Event 4698 Version 0 as inconsistent with Server 2022-era build evidence. The panel agrees that this is a plausible source-schema defect, but limits its weight because it rests on one scheduled-task event and platform inference from a same-host binary version rather than a direct OS-build record.
- The Host/EDR analyst's application/URL mismatches are individually explainable—applications can receive unusual URLs and Postman can call arbitrary APIs—but repeated Webex-to-Slack/Outlook and Postman-to-Snap pairings across independent workstations make a semantics-free destination pool more plausible. The panel therefore keeps the pattern at medium weight rather than treating any one invocation as impossible.

## Most Convincing Evidence

1. All 120 `zeek-core` SMB tree mappings occur an exact integer number of milliseconds after their matching connection starts, preserving the connection's sub-millisecond residue. Review-data recomputation confirmed 120 of 120 exact decimal offsets; other protocol families do not share a collector-wide quantization explanation.
2. Twenty-nine of 32 matched cleartext proxy transactions reportedly have client-facing and origin-facing TCP legs ending within one microsecond despite different starts and durations. The reviewed representative pair ends at exactly `2024-03-18T12:12:08.522962Z` on both legs, which is much more consistent with a shared scheduled close than independent TCP teardown.
3. Twenty-seven of 59 within-connection HTTP intervals fall within 10 microseconds of exactly 600 ms. The verified UID `CdgbJ2WGIwMQpVyyEIN` contains five consecutive exact 600 ms resource-request steps, a strong deterministic scheduler signature.
4. `WS-MCHEN-01` Event 4648 record 949279 places `10.10.1.99` in a local `runas /netonly` chain targeting `DC-01`, although that address is the outbound identity repeatedly used by `LT-MRIVERA-02`; the local workstation's outbound identity is `10.10.1.31`. This is a concrete cross-host ownership leak rather than a completeness complaint.
5. eCAR gives repeated type 7 unlocks new USER_SESSION object IDs while retaining the original `0x6c6a067` logon ID and session ID 1 and pointing back to the original login object. Combined with long-lived reuse of nominally short-lived Postfix worker PIDs and exact Linux daemon quotas reported by the Host/EDR specialist, this supports a broader state-lifecycle and quota-generation defect.

## Most Debated Points

- Whether the SQL SYN-before-process sequence is impossible or merely unattributed. The visible ordering is real and the destination is distinctive, but absent process identity prevents exclusive attribution to PID 7516.
- Whether remote-service logon and SMB source-port mismatches prove a broken continuation. A hidden authentication connection is possible, so the panel requires exact tuple ownership or explicit evidence of the separate channel before escalating this beyond a contract gap.
- How much weight to assign bounded but source-plausible latency distributions. The 41-of-41 SSH authentication floor, 971-of-971 Sysmon-before-Security ordering, 49-of-49 server-process-before-eCAR-flow ordering, and narrow RDP login delay are suspicious in aggregate, but each has a collector, provider, or environment explanation and remains supporting evidence.
- Whether Postfix worker lifetimes require custom-configuration context before they are called implausible. The multi-hour reuse across unrelated transactions is a strong host-specialist finding, but the permitted review-data does not contain the cited syslog files for an independent deliberation check; the panel retains it below the directly verified timing lattices.
- Missing FUIDs, the lone missing STARTTLS SSL row, and the SMB read without a visible open were challenged as invalid missing-coverage claims when considered alone. They remain low-weight quality issues only if the collection/export model claims complete sensor-local companion retention.
- No expert was permitted to use clean attack narration, compactness, cross-source completeness, sanitized domains, filesystem timestamps, or absent source families as authenticity evidence. Such observations were excluded from revised scores and recommendations unless tied to a concrete volume, distribution, ordering, or required-companion defect.

## Improvement Recommendations (Consensus)

1. Decouple independently observed timing at the owning lifecycle and source-timing layers. Give proxy ingress and egress separate response-drain and FIN/ACK timing; derive SMB tree-connect timestamps from packet-stage timing with microsecond variability; replace fixed HTTP resource intervals with dependency-, object-size-, RTT-, cache-, and connection-reuse-aware scheduling; and broaden SSH, RDP, and Windows-provider latency distributions without breaking causality.
2. Repair canonical identity and continuation ownership. Populate Event 4648 network fields from the actual caller context; carry the exact authenticated tuple through remote-service continuations or explicitly emit a distinct authentication connection; and attach failed SQL flows to their real initiating process or leave an observable explanation for a genuinely separate attempt.
3. Preserve one eCAR USER_SESSION identity across login, lock, unlock, and logout. Represent lock and unlock as state transitions on the established session rather than creating new session objects with the same logon and terminal-session identifiers.
4. Make application destinations and dynamic arguments semantics-aware and entity-scoped. Prevent routine Webex/Postman destination assignment from unrelated global pools, and generate `SearchFilterHost.exe`-style runtime handles from host/process state with substantially higher entropy.
5. Model Linux background work from workload and daemon lifecycle rather than per-host quotas. Use realistic creation, reuse, idle timeout, transaction limits, and exit behavior for Postfix workers, while keeping long-lived identities only for appropriate daemons such as `master` and `qmgr`.
6. Select Windows event versions and fields from explicit modeled OS/build capabilities. Emit the Server 2022-appropriate Event 4698 schema and client-process/RPC provenance when that platform is modeled.
7. Preserve the existing strengths: independent sensor identities and clocks, source-native field contracts, capture-loss semantics, DNS TTL aging, TLS chains, EventRecordID reset after log clearing, host-specific paths, role-aware behavior, and bounded-window lifecycle handling. For intentionally missing companions, make source-local loss or abort state coherent and testable rather than forcing universal completeness.
